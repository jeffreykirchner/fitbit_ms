Welcome to the ESI Exercise Study at Chapman University

Key Information

You are being asked to take part in a research study. Research studies include only people who choose to take part. You should take your time to decide whether you want to participate.

If you agree to participate in this study, this research will involve:

  • Individuals who are Chapman University students 18 years or older, signed up in the ESI subject database, and would like to improve their exercise habits.
  • As a participant in this study, you will be provided with a Fitbit device, and you will need to wear it daily to record your physical activity.
  • You may be assigned to a team with another participant whom you choose. Team members will be able to see information about how much physical activity each member does.
  • The study will take place entirely online. It will require a cellphone with Bluetooth capability, online interactions for 1-10 minutes per day, and wearing a Fitbit at least 16 hours a day for the study.
  • Unless otherwise notified, the study will last at most 14 weeks.
  • Payment will be made electronically via PayPal, so you must have a PayPal account linked to your Chapman email address.
  • Risks will not exceed what you would typically encounter in daily life.
  • You will earn $15 for your participation at the initial informational meeting or video, plus $2 a day for each of the first 14 days you participate. After 14 days, the payment schedule will change, and you will be provided with specific instructions about the new schedule. You could earn up to a total of $295 and the Fitbit.

Invitation

You are invited to participate in this research study. The information in this form is meant to help you decide whether to participate. If you have any questions, please ask.

Why are you being asked to be in this research study?

You are being asked to be in this study because you are a Chapman University student who has participated in a previous ESI study where you indicated an interest in improving your exercise habits. You must be 18 years of age or older to participate.

What is the reason for doing this research study?

This research seeks to better understand how (1) participants allocate time between exercise activities and other normal life activities, and (2) what effect monitoring, peers and monetary incentives have. This research is being funded by the Economic Science Institute at Chapman University.

What will be done during this research study?

  • There will be an informational meeting or video prior to the start of the study.
  • The informational meeting or video will provide participation instructions. If you decide to participate, you will be given a Google Health wrist device to monitor your physical activity which you must wear for at least 16 hours per day for the study.
  • You will need to install the Google Health app on your cellphone and link your Google Health through the app. You must then grant Google Health data permission to the study via the study website.
  • You will be required to access the study's website and synchronize your Google Health data once daily. This will require 1-10 minutes and can be completed on your phone.
  • You may be assigned to a team, and your activity data will be shared with your team.
  • The study will last at most 14 weeks.
  • You may be asked to fill out occasional brief questionnaires on the study website, including one at the study's end.

How will my data be used?

Participating in this study requires a Google Health account. Google Health uses cookies and collects your IP address, information about your device, friends, GPS location, purchases, and account and contact information. They use it to stay in touch with you, provide you with information about your health habits and for their own research and development. With your permission, we will get data from them about your device, profile (name, age), weight, heart rate and exercise.

The data of everyone in the study will be analyzed by researchers affiliated with Chapman University to examine factors associated with initiating and sustaining healthy exercise habits. Your data will be anonymized, so that you cannot be personally identified by the data analysts.
